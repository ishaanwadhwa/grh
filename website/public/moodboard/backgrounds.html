Light in the margins

Six ways to warm the empty sides of the page, each taken from the room itself: the candles, the haveli's lime plaster and old brick, the gingham, the arches, the photos' own glow. The centre stays clean and white. Each shows the page at a glance and the left margin at full size, where the texture actually lives. Switch them live on /experiment under Options, Background.

Candlelight

Two warm pools of light just off the page, breathing slowly like candles. No texture, only light. The quietest. Open
