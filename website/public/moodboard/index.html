The room

Candlelight does the art direction.

Decisions

One page. Booking first.

  1. Restaurant-first, phone-first home.Angel is the default; a switcher top-right swaps to Goodroom. No “The Houses / Housemakers / Events” menu up front.
  2. Book a table within one thumb-reach.Hero has two buttons: Book a table (kiss red) and See the menu. A sticky bottom bar keeps Book a table on screen while you scroll.
  3. Two steps, then done.1 When: party, date chips (Tonight, Tomorrow, next 12 days), live time slots, all on one screen. 2 You: name, phone (WhatsApp on), email optional, occasion chips. Then “It's a date.”
  4. No table or area picking in v1.Slots don't carry zones, the room is small, and every extra choice costs bookings. Staff seat people from the floor view.
  5. Menu as the relationship stages.Swipeable stages with 2 or 3 dishes each and prices; the full menu opens in a linen sheet. It's their best copy, so it gets real estate.
  6. Social as proof, not decoration.Six real posts, the handle, and the 5.0 rating. Then: address in Chelon ki Haveli, hours till midnight, Directions, WhatsApp.
  7. The experiment lives on its own route.The current site stays untouched until we choose to swap.

Not this

Magazine cosplayNo drop caps, no ruled columns, no tiny caps label above every heading.
Stock photographyIf it's not their room, their plate or their people, it doesn't go on the site.
Booking as a form pageNo 9-field page behind a “Reserve” link. It's two short steps from anywhere.
