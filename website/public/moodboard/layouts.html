Home layouts, side by side

Three ways to lay out the home page, each live in a desktop frame and a phone frame. They share the same content and booking flow. Scroll inside a frame and tap Book a table to try it in that layout. References from Awwwards are at the bottom.

A

Menu card

A centred column on warm white, like the top of a printed menu, with real photos placed around it and fading in as you scroll. The calmest; booking sits in the middle of the first screen. Open full size

B

Editorial

The classic restaurant-site layout, done carefully: everything hangs off one left edge on a 12-column grid. The name and the line, then one wide photo with two smaller ones stacked beside it, then the story starting on column five. Nothing floats. On phones it is one clean column. Open full size

C

Immersive

The first screen is the room itself: a full-bleed candlelit photo with the name and two buttons over it. As you scroll, the photo pulls back into a framed card and the name lands on the page below it. The most atmospheric; the most like walking in. Open full size

Where these come from

Awwwards restaurant and food sites worth a look (HM is Honorable Mention, SOTD is Site of the Day). Ideas to borrow, not looks to copy.

  • Enoteca della Valpolicella, Verona, HM 2025. A cream menu-card intro that grows into the page; one tilted photo per numbered chapter. Closest to A. Awwwards
  • Studenterkilden, Copenhagen, HM 2026. A centred, all-type first screen with Book a table in a warm colour on every screen size. A. Awwwards
  • Gucci Osteria, Florence, SOTD 2021. Ruled lines like a ledger and arch-shaped photo masks, an echo of the haveli's arches. B. Awwwards
  • Da Pietro 1955, Como, HM 2025. A night photo of a candlelit table with a full-width "Reserve your table" bar on its bottom edge. C. Awwwards
  • Caffè Gilli, Florence, HM 2026. A full-width photo that shrinks into a framed one as you scroll: the zoom-out in C and the Instagram strip. C. Awwwards
  • Flora, Jesolo, HM 2024. Booking as a pop-up on the same page, like our sheet. Awwwards
  • Da Maria, Rome (a concept template), HM 2026. A paper-style booking card with call and email above a short form. Awwwards
