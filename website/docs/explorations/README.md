# Design explorations (2026-10-18)

These are static HTML studies made while designing the Angel and the Searchers page (`src/app/experiment`). Open them straight from disk. They use the photos in `public/images/angel/`.

| Folder | What |
|---|---|
| `hero-variations/` | Ten hero and wordmark treatments: script, serif and Caslon names, a cupid and a searcher drawn into the letters, snapshots behind the type. `angel.png` and `searcher.png` are generated drawings, for exploration only. |
| `layout-c/` | "Layout C": a candlelit photo hero with the name over the room. Includes the text-centring fix. |

More studies live in `public/moodboard/` (`index.html`, `layouts.html`, `options.html`), served at `/moodboard/…` in dev.
